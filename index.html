Still unsure? Message Lena on WhatsApp — she answers in English or German.

Do I need a German credit score (SCHUFA)?−

SCHUFA is Germany's credit bureau, and newcomers don't have a record yet. No law requires one to rent. Many landlords accept an employment contract, payslips or bank statements instead — we focus on those landlords and prepare your documents.
